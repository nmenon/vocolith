This is a technical training session, not a decision-making meeting.

Identify the presenter (the person doing most of the teaching) and populate
extra.presenter with their name as a plain string.

Populate extra.learning_objectives as a list of 3-6 bullet strings describing
what the presenter intended attendees to learn from this session. Derive these
from the opening framing and the topics covered — do not invent them.

Populate extra.discussion_sections as a list of objects, one per major topic
or module covered during the training. Each object must have:
  "title": short topic label (same as its entry in agenda_items)
  "points": list of 3-8 concise bullet strings — technical facts, constraints,
            numbers, and concepts the presenter explained under that topic.
Use only facts stated in the transcript; do not pad with background knowledge.

Populate extra.tools_introduced as a list of strings describing any tools,
scripts, frameworks, workflows, or processes the presenter demonstrated or
announced. Include the name, purpose, and current status if mentioned
(e.g. "internal only", "cloud plugin available", "in development").

Populate extra.qa_highlights as a list of objects for notable Q&A exchanges.
Each object must have:
  "asker": name of the person who asked (use "Attendee" if unknown)
  "question": the question asked, paraphrased concisely
  "answer": the presenter's answer, paraphrased concisely
Only include exchanges with substantive technical or practical content.
Skip trivial acknowledgements or off-topic side comments.

For action_items: capture only explicit commitments made during the session
(e.g. "send link to tool", "share slides", "follow up with customer").
Do not invent action items from the training content itself.

Keep agenda_items and discussion_sections focused on the teaching content,
not on housekeeping or off-topic chatter.
